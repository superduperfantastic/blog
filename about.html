About

Hello world. I’m Tinna. Welcome to my corner of the internet.

I’m based in San Francisco in what was meant to be a “starter home,” though in this city it’s looking more like a forever home. By day, I’m a number cruncher and Excel enthusiast at a large tech company.

Outside of work, I’m usually dreaming about my next travel destination or planning a small house project.

On weekends and days off, you’ll find me volunteering, learning something new, sipping a good beverage, getting outdoors, eating my way around the world, and documenting travels and other adventures here.

Years ago, I underwent major jaw surgery. That journey is documented on this blog as well.

You may also notice references to a former long term partner in older posts. We’re still friends, though you’ll see fewer mentions going forward.
